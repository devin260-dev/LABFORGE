How it works

No drawings or specs needed. Tell me what's not working and I'll handle the rest.

  1. Describe the problem

    A missing cap, a holder that doesn't exist, a rack that's almost right.

  2. I measure on site

    I come to your bench and measure the bottles, tubes or equipment the part has to fit.

  3. You approve a quote

    A fixed price based on the package, agreed before anything gets printed.

  4. Prototype until it's right

    I print prototypes and you test them in real use. I keep adjusting until the fit is right.

  5. Pay the invoice

    A Square invoice arrives by email. Pay online by card. The 1-year reprint starts on delivery.
